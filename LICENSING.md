# Licensing

AI Foundation (`ns_t3af`) is free and open source under **GPL-2.0-or-later**,
published on the TYPO3 Extension Repository and on public GitHub.

There is no price, no licence key, no registration, no domain limit and no time
limit. Install it and it runs, on any domain, production included:

    composer req nitsan/ns-t3af

You may read, fork, modify and redistribute the source under the terms of the GPL.

## Does it call home?

No. AI Foundation makes no licence call and sends us nothing.

## Where your AI content goes

**Bring your own keys, the default.** Your content goes from your server directly
to the AI provider you configured, using your own API key. That provider bills
you. No T3Planet server sits in the data path. You can also run entirely locally
through Ollama and use no external AI service at all.

**AI Credits, optional.** A prepaid balance bought from T3Planet, for teams who
would rather not manage provider API keys. In that mode we route the request, so
the statement above does not apply to it. It is never required and it is not the
default.

## The rest of the AI Universe is commercial

AI Foundation is free. The extensions that build on it (AI Assistant, AI Chatbot,
AI Search, AI Accessibility, AI Localization, AI Builder) are premium products.
Free foundation, paid extensions.
